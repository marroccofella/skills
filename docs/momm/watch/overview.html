0:17 · What MOMM means

MOMM stands for Mixture of Model Modality. Think different AI reviewers, working with different kinds of input: code, written plans, and, where a reviewer supports it, images or documents. It's not a new model. It's a way to make your existing tools work together.

0:34 · Challenge the blind spots

The benefit is broader scrutiny. Ask other tools to challenge an assumption, spot an overlooked risk, or suggest a simpler approach before you commit to it. Your current agent remains responsible for investigating and testing the advice. You get more challenge, without handing your project to competing editors.

0:53 · Less coordination. Parallel review.

If you're manually copying work between chats, you wait for answers, compare the replies, then explain the changes back to your agent. MOMM runs selected reviews in parallel and collects them into one report. That's less manual coordination. It can reduce review waiting, although more reviewers still consume more quota.
