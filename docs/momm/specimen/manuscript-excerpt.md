Title: Ambient blue light exposure improves next-day working memory in adults

## 2. Methods

2.1 Participants. Twelve volunteers (n = 12; 9 women, 4 men; mean age 24.1 years) were recruited from the authors' laboratory group. All participants were graduate students in the same department. No participant was excluded.

2.2 Design. Participants were assigned to either the blue-light condition (a 460 nm LED panel at 300 lux for 60 minutes before sleep) or the control condition (room light, 300 lux) according to the order in which they enrolled: the first six enrollees formed the blue-light group and the remaining six the control group. The following morning, participants completed a 2-back working-memory task, a digit-span task, a Stroop task, a simple reaction-time task, a verbal fluency task and a self-reported sleep-quality questionnaire.

2.3 Analysis. Group differences on each of the six outcome measures were tested with independent-samples t-tests (two-tailed, alpha = 0.05). Because the measures address distinct cognitive domains, no correction for multiple comparisons was applied. Analyses were performed in a spreadsheet; the raw data are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.

## 3. Results

Blue-light participants scored higher on the 2-back task than controls (mean accuracy 81.3% vs 74.9%; t(10) = 2.27, p = 0.047). Differences on the digit-span (p = 0.31), Stroop (p = 0.62), reaction-time (p = 0.19) and verbal-fluency (p = 0.71) tasks did not reach significance. Self-reported sleep quality was lower in the blue-light group (p = 0.08), which we interpret as evidence that blue light consolidates memory through mild sleep restriction.

## 4. Discussion

These results demonstrate that a single hour of evening blue-light exposure causes a clinically meaningful improvement in working memory in adults. The effect size (Cohen's d = 0.31) indicates a large effect. Because the 2-back task is the gold-standard measure of executive function, we recommend that blue-light panels be adopted in workplace wellness programmes pending replication. The 14% relative improvement we observed (81.3 vs 74.9) is consistent with prior reports of 3-5% improvements from morning light.

Data availability: raw data available on request. Preregistration: none. Funding: the LED panel was supplied by its manufacturer.
