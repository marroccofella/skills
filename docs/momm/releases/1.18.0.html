What 1.18 is

MOMM 1.18 adds Bumblers to the review pool. A Bumbler is a lower-powered, usually open-source model you run yourself. It joins a review by name, for example --reviewers codex,antigravity,bab, and goes through the same prompt, process containment, validation, evidence and statuses as every other reviewer. Bab, a model on the owner's own machine, is the first Bumbler.

  • Setup: add one in the Setup Center under Bumblers in the review pool, or in bumblers.json in your reviewer home. See getting started.
  • Findings: what a Bumbler raises reaches the governor, who must decide it. If its quotes fail validation, its findings are still shown, marked quotes_unverified, and its row says invalid_output.
  • Pool and quorum: a saved Bumbler joins every review unless you name reviewers, and a valid review counts towards --min-success like any other. An invalid review gives no vote. With --strict, a named Bumbler must answer.
  • Access: a Bumbler route has no login and no API key. Review text goes to the saved address, which must be a tailnet or loopback address. That address is reachability, not authentication, so add only a machine you trust.
  • Limits: tokens, time, an optional rest and how many pieces it takes at once (parallel, 1 by default, up to 8) are set per Bumbler. A shared server that answers 429 is waited on as it asks, within one fixed deadline; wait_when_busy: false asks once.
  • Own guidance: each Bumbler can carry up to 4000 characters of its own reviewing style on top of MOMM's general guidance.

Known quality: on 2 November Bab reviewed 8 real diffs from this repository, 4 clean and 4 with a seeded bug, with her own guidance. She reached the right verdict on 5 of 8: 3 of 4 bugs and 2 of 4 clean diffs, with 6 valid reviews. Some right verdicts carried a wrong diagnosis, and some repair advice was wrong. On small synthetic files with a stated contract she reached 9 of 9. Treat every Bumbler finding as the governor treats any reviewer's: reproduce it.

Known speed: Bab held about 21 tokens per second for her first 5 minutes of load, then fell to 5.7, which caused timeouts. When requests were paused 90 seconds apart from outside MOMM, she stayed at 17 to 21 with no timeouts, and all seven answers that could be compared matched the unpaused run byte for byte. That shows the pause coincided with full speed, not why she slows. rest_seconds now does this pausing inside MOMM; it is tested against a stand-in server, not yet on her. Other modest GPUs may behave differently. With two jobs at once on her two 64k slots, each ran at about 12.5 tokens a second while they overlapped, about 25 combined. parallel lets one Bumbler take that many pieces at once, and a shared server that answers 429 is waited on and asked again within the time limit. In a three-piece review with parallel 2 and max_tokens 4096, one piece answered (1266 tokens at 14 tokens a second) and two were cancelled at the time limit, with no token count for those. At that rate a 4096-token answer would need about 290 seconds, so long answers risk the limit. MOMM marked her review partial. In the final review she ran with max_tokens 6144, 590 seconds, parallel 2 and no rest, at the owner's request: she slowed to about 5 tokens a second under sustained load, two pieces timed out, and one of her 17 pieces gave a valid review.

Final review: run rev_20261102161835_b21cc084fe09 reviewed the Bumbler code with Codex, Antigravity, Copilot, Grok and Bab; quorum was met on all 17 pieces and the governor recorded it complete.
