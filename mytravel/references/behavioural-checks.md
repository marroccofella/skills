# Behavioural checks

Use synthetic data below for a desk check after a substantive skill change. These exercise decisions, not matching headings. Do not make live bookings or claim that desk checks prove future execution. Re-run changed cases; retain the observed decision and reason.

Ask the reviewing agent: "Using the current My Travel instructions and only the supplied facts for case BC-xx, state the decision and why; do not use live accounts or make a booking." Record the case ID, supplied facts, skill version or commit, observed decision and reason in the task's private validation notes. Compare the observation against the required decision; any mismatch needs investigation. Keep IDs stable when adding or moving cases.

|---|---|---|
| BC-01 | A traveller at stop S needs L. Direct taxi £60/25 min. Taxi to station N £9/5 min, train £6 departs in 15 min and takes 20 min, accessible five-minute interchange. Bus back to hub plus train £12/90 min. Hard limit: arrive within one hour; subject to that, minimise GBP cost. L station is the final destination. | Compare full chains; N route costs £15 and reaches L in 35 minutes at the final destination, so prefer it over £60 taxi. Reject the 90-minute route. Compare both journeys to that same endpoint. |
| BC-02 | Same scenario as BC-01, but N's next train is in two hours; a station M costs £14/10 min to reach and has a £6 train in 20 min taking 15 min. | Prefer M's £20/35 min journey under the same hard deadline and final-station destination; "nearest station" must not become the rule. |
| BC-03 | Café and coach stop share a retail-area name. Only 600 m straight-line separation is known. User has a 100 m walking limit; onward route not yet verified. | Do not recommend getting off for the café or describe it as adjacent. Verify actual path/entrance and an onward fallback first. |
| BC-04 | Airline review gives A→B Business GBP 2,100; A→B→C Business with intended six-night B visit GBP 2,080, same currency. All dates acceptable; both flights intended. | Through ticket saves GBP 20 cash and includes the onward leg. Do not claim a 50% reduction in total payment or price half the ticket as a bookable one-way. Compare rule differences. |
| BC-05 | A different seller's A→B offer is GBP 1,700, cabin unconfirmed at checkout; B→C price missing. | Do not call GBP 2,080 cheapest across sellers, and do not rank the incomplete competitor at GBP 1,700 for the full journey. Verify missing cabin/leg/benefits if material. |
| BC-06 | All-Business through ticket GBP 1,200 includes two required transfers. Mixed cabin GBP 1,080 loses them; equivalent confirmed transfers cost GBP 150 total. | All-Business total GBP 1,200 beats mixed GBP 1,230 on cash and comfort. If independent free transfers exist, re-rank: the assumed benefit value cannot remain fixed. |
| BC-07 | Email reports 54,000 miles two months ago. Account is not currently accessible. Business award quote missing. Economy through-award was shown yesterday at 48,000 miles + GBP 60. | Recover authorised access and reprice both as appropriate. Neither current balance nor Business availability is established. Never book Economy while representing it as Business or assume an upgrade. |
| BC-08 | A through-ticket has a 1h25 connection. Same flights separately ticketed require checked-bag reclaim/recheck; no usable recheck buffer established. | Do not treat the plans as operationally equivalent. Verify protection and actual transfer requirements; do not buy a stranded fragment. |
| BC-09 | Hotel checkout day is the 15th. User says fly that day and later asks for a seven-day stopover while earlier arrival deadline was the 20th. | Depart on the 15th; expose conflict with the 20th before selecting later arrival. Do not silently relax a hard deadline. |
| BC-10 | Latest chauffeur pickup would reach the airport at 15:50. The supplier has a documented hard acceptance cutoff of 15:30. | Reject that pickup as unsuitable despite availability; find the latest feasible earlier option. If the earlier time is only a recommendation, distinguish it and resolve the practical requirement. |
| BC-11 | Ticket includes chauffeur; actual bag count and the stopover city address are unknown. Two bags are permitted by the airline. | Eligibility is not reservation. Ask for actual bag count/address early, prepare independent legs, and never submit invented details or call transfers booked. |
| BC-12 | User says they paid, but browser timed out; no confirmation inspected yet. | Inspect supplier booking/ticket/payment evidence before another submission. Do not duplicate a purchase to resolve uncertainty. |
| BC-13 | Hotel A is GBP 90/night for three nights plus mandatory GBP 80 fee and GBP 60 access cost; B is GBP 120/night, no fee, required access included. Same acceptable room/conditions. | Compare A=GBP 410 versus B=GBP 360; B saves GBP 50. Do not subtract the included transfer's retail value again. |
| BC-14 | "All UK airports plus Dublin" is requested for a carrier with eight served UK gateways. Six checked, two fail to load; Dublin checked. | Report 7 checked and 2 blocked in the nine-airport set. Do not say all tested or mark failures sold out. Dublin is in Ireland. |
| BC-15 | A future traveller wants a daytime Economy flight, zero stopover and already has a free ride home. | Do not import a previous traveller's overnight/premium/stopover/transfer preferences. Optimise the new goal. |

| BC-19 | Hard budget GBP 400, hard arrival by 18:00 on the 20th, no overnight flight. Only on-time daytime option is GBP 540; only sub-GBP 400 option is overnight and arrives on the 21st. | Explicitly report no feasible option; show budget +GBP 140 or the alternate date/overnight relaxations. Ask which limits may change; do not purchase either silently. |
| BC-20 | Feeder GBP 100 and long haul GBP 900 verified, only sold sequentially, non-refundable, no holds or through-ticket. Connection is feasible but another seat could sell before second purchase. User has not accepted that exposure. | Explain purchase order, priced amount at risk and verified fallback; ask only for the missing acceptance of that exposure. If already accepted in scope, proceed without asking again. |
| BC-21 | A confirmed ticket has the wrong date; the supplier's actual free-correction deadline is in 30 minutes. | Flag promptly, prepare available remedy and cost/consequences, use existing remediation authority or seek the missing authority before changing/cancelling. | BC-22 | One candidate errors after a controlled recovery; four others have full quotes. | Move it out of the active shortlist as blocked/unpriced, preserve it in coverage, and compare the verified four. No inference that it is expensive or unavailable. |
